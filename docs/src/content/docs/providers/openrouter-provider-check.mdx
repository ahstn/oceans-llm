This page is a point-in-time check of the upstream providers behind one OpenRouter model, `deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017`. It lists each provider's headquarters and the compliance audits it publishes. Admins can use it to choose provider slugs for a route's [OpenRouter provider routing](/providers/openrouter/#set-openrouter-provider-routing) policy.

## Scope and method

- Checked on 2026-11-22. OpenRouter listed 40 providers across 50 endpoints for the model.
- Headquarters comes from OpenRouter's `/api/v1/providers` data, checked against each company's own pages and filings.
- Audit claims come from each company's trust center, blog and legal pages, plus the third-party trackers linked below. Each trust center keeps its audit reports behind a request, and this check didn't open any of them.
- The table lists US-headquartered providers that publish a SOC 2 Type II audit or claim one.
  - **Tier 1**: SOC 2 Type II, plus ISO certification and/or HIPAA coverage.
  - **Tier 2**: SOC 2 Type II only, or a Type II claim without a date or a posted report.

## Providers to avoid

OpenRouter and the providers disclosed these data-handling incidents in November 2026. They involve other models on these providers, not DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017. OpenRouter's [incident summary](https://openrouter.ai/blog/announcements/data-processing-incidents-on-inference-providers/) says it off-boarded three providers, removed 125 endpoints from Zero Data Retention eligibility and notified affected customers.

| Provider | What happened | Sources |
|---|---|---|

A researcher's test URLs in MiniMax-model prompts were opened 40 to 130 hours after the requests. | [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/blog/announcements/data-processing-incidents-on-inference-providers/) · [Customer notice on X](https://x.com/michael_chomsky/status/2124008002571428224) |
| Novita AI | Forwarded MiniMax-model requests to MiniMax's API. Novita says a routing configuration issue applied the forwarding path more broadly than intended, from 2026-05-28 to 2026-11-11. | [Novita notice](https://novita.ai/blog/minimax-requests-privacy-notice/) · [Customer notice on X](https://x.com/michael_chomsky/status/2124008002571428224) |
| StreamLake | Told OpenRouter it forwarded OpenRouter requests for MiniMax models to MiniMax. | [Customer notice on X](https://x.com/michael_chomsky/status/2124008002571428224) |

## Providers to consider avoiding

Users may want to avoid OpenInference, Nebius and Baidu for this model. In a [bugbunny.ai test](https://x.com/Zaddyzaddy/status/2123810047163539875) on 2026-11-19, their endpoints drifted furthest from DeepSeek's own API: 20.4%, 13.2% and 11.8%. All other endpoints measured under 10%, and none reached the test's 50% threshold for a different model. OpenInference and Nebius declare fp4 quantization on OpenRouter, and Baidu declares fp8.

The test sent 40 one-word prompts in four languages, 65 samples per endpoint, and measured Jensen-Shannon distance from DeepSeek's API, scaled so DeepSeek V4-Pro equals 100%. The method follows [One Token Is Enough](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10252).

## Use the slugs in a route

The OpenRouter slug column matches the slugs that `compatibility.openrouter.provider.only` accepts:

```yaml title="gateway.yaml"
models:
  - id: deepseek-v4.1-flash
    routes:
      - provider: openrouter
        upstream_model: deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017
        compatibility:
          openrouter:
            provider:
              only: [fireworks, crusoe, together, baseten]
```

Provider audits and OpenRouter's provider list change over time. Check a provider's trust center before relying on an entry here.
